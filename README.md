# Welcome to the Silicon critters mod

This is a Minecraft Infection mod for **Minecraft Forge 1.20.1** that adds many hungry and dangerous critters to your world. Unlike ours, their biology is based on Silicon, which gives them unique properties!

Unlike most infection mods, this one is very fast paced - these critters evolve right in front of your eyes. They might seem weak on first sight, but getting rid of them ain't a simple task.
Oh and did I tell you that they love fire and warmth? Keep your flamethrowers far away from these guys!

This mod also adds a global warming system, which increases the atmospheric temperature the more these critters evolve, allowing them to become faster, stronger and smarter!

Chemosynthesis will most-likely wreak havoc upon your existing minecraft world, so be careful when adding this to your modpack build!
